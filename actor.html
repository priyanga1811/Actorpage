Vijay

Joseph Vijay Chandrasekhar, popularly known as **Thalapathy Vijay**, is one of the biggest stars in Tamil cinema, born on 22 June 1974 in Chennai. He began his career as a child artist and rose to fame as a leading actor with films like *Ghilli*, *Pokiri*, *Thuppakki*, *Kaththi*, *Mersal*, *Master*, and *Leo*, becoming known for his strong screen presence, energetic dance, mass appeal, and socially relevant themes. Apart from acting, he is also a playback singer and a philanthropist, widely admired for his simplicity and connection with fans through Vijay Makkal Iyakkam. In 2024, Vijay formally entered politics by launching his party, **Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam (TVK)**, marking a new phase in his public life.
